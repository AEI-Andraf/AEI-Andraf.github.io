Project 10: Russian Media Monitor (since August 2026)

An automated monitoring pipeline for Russian-language media coverage of Russia. Every day it collects press, Telegram channels, television programmes, YouTube channels and VKontakte communities — 55+ sources across five media types, each tagged by editorial family (state, para-state, independent, exile) and legal status. Television and video are transcribed with Whisper, contents are clustered with BERTopic, and a Mistral model scores geopolitical sentiment, lexical framing and rhetorical devices. Everything lands in DuckDB and is served through a Streamlit dashboard of nine analytical tabs.

Project 9: Election Clustering – Worldwide Legislative Electoral Systems (December 2025)

Analysis of lower-chamber legislative election results using constituency-level data (CLEA). Pipeline includes preprocessing of missing codes, standard scaling, PCA, t-SNE/UMAP embeddings, and clustering with KMeans, DBSCAN, and HDBSCAN. The report includes a focused case study on Russian elections (2003 vs 2016).

Project 2: Drone Classification in Ukraine using Machine Learning (April 2024)

In the context of modern warfare, the use of military drones in Ukraine has become a major strategic issue. This project focuses on applying machine learning to classify different types of drones, whether for reconnaissance or as attack weapons. Real-time classification enhances the ability to respond to enemy strategies. The project began with an OSINT phase to gather coordinates of Russian bases and technical specifications of drones. A Random Forest model was trained to classify 1000 simulated drone trajectories. This work contributes to the broader transformation of warfare through AI and automation.

Project 1: Syria Civil War Analysis (February 2024)

The Syrian civil war, which began in 2011, is one of the most complex conflicts of the 21st century. This project written in French explores the deep-rooted causes of the conflict, highlighting the repression of an authoritarian regime, internal power struggles, and foreign interventions. It includes a detailed analysis of key actors such as the Assad regime, rebel groups, and jihadist factions. The war’s impact on the region and international relations is central, especially regarding tensions with global powers. As Syria remains a strategic crossroads in the Middle East, this project aims to clarify the underlying dynamics that continue to destabilize the region.
